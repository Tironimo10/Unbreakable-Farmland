# Unbreakable Farmland

## Overview
**Unbreakable Farmland** makes your farmland completely jumpproof! Say goodbye to accidentally destroying your crops when jumping or falling on farmland.

## Features
- 🌱 **Complete Protection**: Prevents farmland from being trampled by players and mobs
- 🔧 **Zero Configuration**: Works instantly after installation
- 🖥️ **Server Friendly**: Works in both singleplayer and multiplayer environments
- 📦 **Lightweight**: Simple implementation with minimal performance impact

## How It Works
The mod prevents the vanilla trampling mechanic without changing any other aspects of farming or movement. Jump, run, or fall on your farmland without fear - your crops will remain intact!

## Compatibility
- Works with all crop types
- No known conflicts with other mods

## Installation
1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/)
2. Download Unbreakable Farmland
3. Place the .jar file in your `mods` folder
4. Enjoy your jumpproof farmland!
